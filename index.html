Exam Information

Bangladeshi Education System

Key exams, boards, and what to expect at each level

📝

SSC — Secondary School Certificate

Class 10 board exam. Conducted by 9 education boards. Results typically in May.

Class 10
🎓

HSC — Higher Secondary Certificate

Class 12 board exam. Key for university admissions. Results announced after October.

Class 12
🏛️

University Admission Tests

DU, BUET, CU, RU, JU — individual admission tests for public universities.

After HSC
🏫

PSC / JSC / JDC

Primary and junior school certificates. Now reformed into continuous assessment.

Class 5 & 8
🏆

BCS — Bangladesh Civil Service

BPSC conducts BCS exam for government service. Highly competitive, multi-stage.

After Graduation
✈️

IELTS / Study Abroad Tests

IELTS, GRE, SAT, TOEFL — required for scholarships and foreign university applications.

Study Abroad

Financial Aid

Scholarships for Bangladeshi Students

Opportunities at home and abroad — fully funded & partial

🇧🇩 Bangladesh

Prime Minister's Education Assistance Trust

Government fund for meritorious and financially needy students from SSC level to university.

Fully Funded SSC–University Need-based
🇯🇵 Japan

MEXT Japanese Government Scholarship

Full scholarship for undergraduate, master's, and PhD in Japan. Covers tuition, living costs, and flights.

Fully Funded All Levels Deadline: May
🇬🇧 UK

Commonwealth Scholarship

For master's and PhD studies in the UK. Covers tuition, travel, and living expenses. Competitive selection.

Fully Funded Masters & PhD
🇺🇸 USA

Fulbright Foreign Student Program

For master's and PhD study in the US. Requires strong academic record and English proficiency.

Fully Funded Masters & PhD Merit-based
🇧🇩 Bangladesh

National Science & Technology Fellowship

For science & engineering students at masters and PhD level. Monthly stipend + research funding.

Funded STEM Masters/PhD
🇩🇪 Germany

DAAD Scholarship Program

Germany's largest funding program for international students. Many programs are in English. No tuition at public universities.

Partially Funded All Levels
🇧🇩 Bangladesh

Upazila Level Stipend Program

Government stipend for girl students and underprivileged students at secondary level. Contact local upazila office.

Stipend SSC Level Need-based
🇨🇳 China

Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC)

Fully-funded bachelor's, master's, and PhD in China. Many programs in English. Very competitive for Bangladeshi students.

Fully Funded All Levels Deadline: March
🇹🇷 Turkey

Türkiye Bursları (Turkey Scholarships)

Full scholarship for bachelor's, masters, and PhD in Turkey. Includes stipend, accommodation, health insurance.

Fully Funded All Levels February Deadline
